Google AdSense setup

1. Replace REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_PUBLISHER_ID in ALL five HTML files (index, learning, lessons, quiz, donate) AND in ads.txt with your real Google AdSense publisher ID.
   Example: pub-1234567890123456

2. In AdSense, add your domain and request review.

3. After approval, create ad units or enable Auto ads.

Important:
- Keep the donation page clean (no aggressive ad placement near the crypto address).
- ads.txt must stay at the root of your domain.
